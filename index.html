Speaking & consulting

Two kinds of conversation

For cruise lines

Enrichment that lands

Twenty years of watching what works in a ship's theatre and what empties it. I speak on board, and I help plan enrichment programmes that match the itinerary, the ship and the guests actually sailing.

  • Destination talks, port talks, sailing commentaries
  • Programme design by itinerary
  • Speaker briefing & content standards

For cruisers

Before you sail

A conversation about your specific itinerary. Which ports reward planning and which reward wandering, what to book ahead, what the local etiquette is, and enough language to be welcomed rather than tolerated.

  • Itinerary review, port by port
  • Cultural briefing & etiquette
  • Survival language, if you want it
