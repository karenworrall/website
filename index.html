The Look So Far

The design carries over from the talks site: deep sea blues warming to lagoon and turquoise, an antique gold hairline for emphasis, and a warm off-white page rather than a stark white one.

Headings are set in Cormorant Garamond, a serif with a literary feel that suits a writer. Body text is Jost, a clean geometric sans that keeps long reads comfortable. The wave motif at the foot of the header is the one recurring flourish.
